What a private cook does

  • Planning weekly menus with the family
  • Grocery shopping
  • Preparing meals in your kitchen, including meal prep for the week
  • Following dietary needs: allergies, kids' meals, low-carb, vegetarian
  • Cleaning the kitchen after cooking

What to ask before you hire

  • Which cuisines they cook (Brazilian, Latin, American, other)
  • Experience with your dietary needs or allergies
  • Whether they shop for groceries and how they'll be reimbursed
  • How many meals per visit and how many hours
  • References and, ideally, a trial meal

Hiring legally in Florida

A cook who works in your home on your schedule is usually your household employee. In 2026 Social Security and Medicare taxes apply if you pay $3,000 or more in cash wages in the year (IRS Publication 926). See our guide: how to hire a household employee in Florida.
