Privacy Policy

Last updated November 04, 2026

This policy explains how F Platform LLC ("Famyle"), a Delaware limited liability company collects and uses personal information on us.famyle.com. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Families: first name, email address, mobile number (optional), ZIP code, the type of help you need, schedule, pay you offer and job details. Please don't post children's names or photos.
  • Professionals: name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, services and preferred language; later, if you complete a profile: skills, experience, desired pay, languages, date of birth (to confirm you are 18+) and profile photo.
  • Your consent: whether you agreed to be contacted by WhatsApp, text or email, with the date and the page where you agreed.
  • Automatically: device and usage data, and cookies or pixels that measure our ads (Google Ads).

How we use it

To create your account, match Families with Professionals near them, contact you about your job or applications, keep the Services safe, and improve them. We may use automated tools to help match people; you can always ask a person to review a match.

Texts, WhatsApp and email

We contact you by WhatsApp or text only if you agreed to it, about your job or about jobs near you, between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. local time. Reply STOP at any time to stop. Msg & data rates may apply. Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing.

Who we share it with

Other users see what's needed to connect (for example, a Professional's profile and a Family's job). Service providers process data for us under contract: our cloud hosting and database providers, website hosting (GitHub Pages), analytics and advertising measurement (Google Analytics and Google Ads), and our email and messaging providers. We share data with authorities only when required by law or to prevent harm. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of ad sharing.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it within 90 days after you ask us to delete your account (email suporte@famyle.com), except what we must keep by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and opt out of targeted advertising, by emailing suporte@famyle.com. We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

Children

Famyle is for adults 18+. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Where data is processed

In the United States and in Brazil, where our team and our database are. Famyle in the United States runs on the same platform as Famyle in Brazil, operated by our affiliate under contract.
