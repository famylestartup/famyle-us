Privacy Policy

Last updated November 02, 2026

This policy explains how F Platform LLC ("Famyle"), a Delaware limited liability company collects and uses personal information on us.famyle.com. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • From your Google or Apple sign-in: name, email address and account identifier.
  • Families: ZIP code, the type of help you need, schedule, pay you offer and job details. Please don't post children's names or photos.
  • Professionals: ZIP code, services, skills, experience, desired pay, languages, date of birth (to confirm you are 18+), phone number and profile photo.
  • Automatically: device and usage data, and cookies or pixels that measure our ads (Google Ads).

How we use it

To create your account, match Families with Professionals near them, contact you about your job or applications, keep the Services safe, and improve them. We may use automated tools to help match people; you can always ask a person to review a match.

Who we share it with

Other users see what's needed to connect (for example, a Professional's profile and a Family's job). Service providers process data for us under contract: Google (Firebase hosting, sign-in and database), Apple (sign-in) and our email and messaging providers. We share data with authorities only when required by law or to prevent harm. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of ad sharing.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it within 90 days after you ask us to delete your account (email suporte@famyle.com), except what we must keep by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and opt out of targeted advertising, by emailing suporte@famyle.com. We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

Children

Famyle is for adults 18+. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Where data is processed

In the United States and in Brazil, where part of our team works.
