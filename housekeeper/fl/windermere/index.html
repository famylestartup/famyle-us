What a housekeeper does

  • Regular cleaning of the whole home: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, floors
  • Laundry, ironing and changing bed linens
  • Tidying and organizing
  • Some families also ask for grocery runs or simple meal prep — agree on this upfront

What to ask before you hire

  • Which days and hours you need, and whether it's live-out or live-in
  • Experience with homes like yours (size, pets, children)
  • References from other families you can call
  • Which cleaning products they prefer, and who buys them
  • How they want to be paid and how often

Hiring legally in Florida

A housekeeper who works in your home on a schedule you set is usually your household employee. In 2026, if you pay one household employee $3,000 or more in cash wages in the year, you owe Social Security and Medicare taxes (IRS Publication 926). Florida has no state income tax.
