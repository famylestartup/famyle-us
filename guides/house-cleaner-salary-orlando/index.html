How Much Do House Cleaners Make in Orlando, FL? (2026)

Hourly pay for house cleaners and housekeepers in Orlando, how much a cleaner makes per house, and how independent cleaners set their rates.

Quick answer: House cleaners in Orlando, FL typically charge $20.25–$25.07 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026); the BLS median for maids and housekeeping cleaners in the Orlando metro is $16.90 per hour (May 2025). A regular 3-bedroom cleaning takes 3–4 hours, about $66–$88 per visit at $22 an hour.

How much per house?

A regular cleaning of a 3-bedroom home takes about 3 to 4 hours. At $22 an hour that's about $66–$88 per visit. A cleaner with 2 homes a day, 5 days a week, at about $77 per home earns around $770 a week before costs.

Simple math, not a survey.

Setting your rate as an independent cleaner

  • Charge more for the first visit or a deep clean, which take longer.
  • Weekly and biweekly clients give you a steady schedule.
  • Bringing your own supplies usually means a higher rate.
  • As a self-employed cleaner, you report your income and pay self-employment tax (IRS).
