How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Orlando, FL? (2026)

Hourly rates for house cleaners and housekeepers in Orlando, independent cleaner vs. cleaning company, and how often to book.

Quick answer: House cleaning in Orlando, FL typically costs $20.25–$25.07 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026). A regular cleaning of a 3-bedroom home takes 3–4 hours, so about $70–$90 per visit. Independent cleaners usually cost less than cleaning companies, and most Orlando families book weekly or every two weeks.

Independent cleaner or cleaning company?

  • Independent cleaner: usually costs less, and you get the same person every visit.
  • Cleaning company: handles insurance and sends a replacement if someone is sick, but adds its margin and may send different people.

How long a cleaning takes

A regular cleaning of a 3-bedroom home typically takes 3 to 4 hours; the first visit or a deep clean takes longer. At $22/hour, a 3.5-hour visit is about $77.

Is my house cleaner my employee?

An independent cleaner who runs their own business, brings their own supplies and serves other clients is generally not your household employee (IRS Publication 926). A cleaner who works on your schedule under your direction may be.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip my house cleaner?

Tips aren't required for a regular cleaner you hire directly; many families give a holiday bonus of about one visit's pay.

How often do Orlando families book a cleaner?

Most book every week or every two weeks.
