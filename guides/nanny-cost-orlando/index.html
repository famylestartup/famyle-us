How Much Does a Nanny Cost in Orlando, FL? (2026)

Hourly rates for nannies and babysitters in Orlando, what drives the price, and the taxes families owe when they hire a nanny.

Quick answer: A nanny in Orlando, FL typically costs $20.79–$26.36 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026). Full-time, 40 hours a week, that's about $3,600–$4,600 a month before taxes. If you pay a nanny $3,000 or more in the year, you also owe Social Security and Medicare (7.65%) and report it on Schedule H.

What changes the price

  • Number and ages of children: families usually add $1–$2/hour per extra child.
  • Experience, CPR/first-aid and education in early childhood.
  • Driving: a nanny who drives the kids usually costs more.
  • Languages: bilingual nannies are in demand in Orlando.
  • Full-time vs. occasional: full-time nannies often agree on a weekly salary.

A simple monthly estimate

A full-time nanny at $22/hour for 40 hours a week costs about $3,800 a month before taxes ($22 × 40 × 52 ÷ 12).

Taxes families owe

In 2026, if you pay a nanny $3,000 or more in cash wages in the year, you withhold 7.65% (Social Security and Medicare) from their pay and pay the same 7.65% yourself. If you pay $1,000 or more in a calendar quarter, federal unemployment tax (FUTA) and Florida reemployment tax also apply. You report it all on Schedule H. Florida has no state income tax. Frequently asked questions

How much is a full-time nanny in Orlando per month?

About $3,600 to $4,600 a month for 40 hours a week at the typical Orlando range of $20.79–$26.36/hour (Care.com, November 2026), before employer taxes.

Do I have to pay taxes for my nanny in Florida?

If you pay them $3,000 or more in 2026, yes: Social Security and Medicare (7.65% each side). Florida has no state income tax, but Florida reemployment tax applies if you pay $1,000 or more in a quarter.
