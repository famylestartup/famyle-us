How to choose, step by step

  1. Size the job. Count bedrooms, bathrooms and pets, and list the tasks (kitchen, floors, laundry, inside the fridge or oven).
  2. Pick the frequency. Weekly keeps each visit shorter; every two weeks costs less per month.
  3. Set the pay. Use the calculator and the Orlando range above; offer an hourly rate or a fixed price per visit.
  4. Post your job for free with your ZIP code, schedule and pay. Cleaners near you apply.
  5. Talk to applicants. Ask for references from other families and call them. A first paid cleaning works as a trial.
  6. Agree in writing on tasks, supplies, the price and what happens on holidays and cancellations.

Red flags

  • No price or task list agreed before the first visit.
  • A request to pay in full for cleanings that haven't happened yet, or to pay with gift cards.
  • No references you can actually call.
  • A quote far below the typical range for a large home: ask how many hours it includes.

Independent cleaner or maid service?

A maid service sends its own staff and handles replacements; an independent cleaner is the same person every visit and you pay them directly. See maid service in Orlando for the full comparison.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a weekly house cleaner cost per month in Orlando?

About $307–$380 a month for a 3.5-hour weekly cleaning at the typical Orlando range of $20.25–$25.07 per hour (Care.com, November 2026).

How much is a cleaning every two weeks in Orlando?

About $71–$88 per visit, or $154–$190 a month on average, for 3.5 hours at the same range.

How many hours does a house cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the home, pets and the tasks. Use your own estimate in the calculator; the first visit or a deep clean usually takes longer than a regular one.

Is a house cleaner I hire directly my employee?

An independent cleaner who runs their own business, brings their own supplies and serves other clients is generally not your household employee (IRS Publication 926). A cleaner who works on your schedule under your direction may be.
