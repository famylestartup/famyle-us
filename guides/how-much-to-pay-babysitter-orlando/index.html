What changes the rate

  • More children: families often add $1–$2 an hour per extra child.
  • Late nights and holidays (New Year's Eve, July 4th) usually cost more.
  • Driving, cooking or helping with homework.
  • Experience, CPR certification and languages.

Tips

  • Agree on the rate, the end time and how you'll pay before the first night.
  • Round up to the next half hour, and pay a ride home or rideshare after late nights.
  • Occasional babysitting is usually not a household employee arrangement; a regular weekly schedule may be. See hiring a household employee in Florida.
