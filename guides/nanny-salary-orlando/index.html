How Much Do Nannies Make in Orlando, FL? (2026 Salary Guide)

Hourly, weekly and yearly nanny pay in Orlando, what raises it, and where to find nanny jobs near Winter Garden, Windermere and Dr. Phillips.

Quick answer: Nannies in Orlando, FL typically earn $20.79–$26.36 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026). Full-time at $22 an hour, that's about $880 a week or $45,760 a year before taxes. Live-out nannies are entitled to overtime above 40 hours a week under federal law.

What raises a nanny's pay

  • Experience with the same age group, especially infants.
  • CPR and first-aid certification.
  • A clean driving record and your own car for school runs.
  • Speaking a second language: Portuguese and Spanish are in demand in west Orlando.
  • Caring for more than one child.

Overtime and taxes

Under the federal FLSA, a nanny who doesn't live in the family's home is entitled to overtime above 40 hours a week. If a family pays a nanny $3,000 or more in 2026, they withhold Social Security and Medicare and give the nanny a W-2 (IRS Publication 926).

Frequently asked questions

How much does a nanny make per hour in Orlando?

Families in Orlando typically pay nannies $20.79–$26.36 an hour (Care.com, November 2026). The BLS median for all childcare workers in the Orlando metro, including daycare staff, is $15.00 an hour (May 2025).

How much does a full-time nanny make a year in Orlando?

At $22 an hour for 40 hours a week, about $45,760 a year before taxes.

Do nannies get overtime in Florida?

Yes, a nanny who doesn't live with the family is entitled to overtime above 40 hours a week under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act.
