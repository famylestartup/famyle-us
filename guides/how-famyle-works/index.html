How Does Famyle Work? A Guide for Orlando Families and Professionals

Step by step: how families post a job and hire a nanny, housekeeper or pet sitter on Famyle, how professionals find work, what's free, and what Famyle does and does not do.

Quick answer: Famyle connects families in west Orlando with local household professionals. A family posts a job for free, professionals nearby apply, and the family talks to them and hires directly, agreeing on pay and schedule. Professionals join and apply for free. Famyle is not an employer or agency and does not run background checks.

Famyle has helped families in Brazil find household help for more than 10 years. In Orlando we start in the west side of town: Winter Garden and Horizon West, Windermere, Dr. Phillips and MetroWest. We cover housekeepers, house cleaners, nannies, babysitters, pet sitters and private cooks.

For families: step by step

  1. Post your job. On the Famyle home page, say what you need (for example, a nanny for two kids after school), your area, the days and hours. It takes a few minutes and costs nothing.
  2. Professionals nearby apply. Your job is shown to professionals who live close to you, so they spend less time driving.
  3. Talk to the candidates. You talk to each professional directly: call, video chat or meet in person. Ask about experience, availability and references.
  4. Check them yourself. Call references, ask for a paid trial day and, if you want a background check, follow the rules in our background check guide.
  5. Hire directly. You and the professional agree on pay, schedule and duties, ideally in writing. You pay the professional directly; Famyle takes no cut.

For professionals: step by step

  1. Join for free on the professionals page and tell us what you do and where you live.
  2. See jobs near you on the jobs page and apply to the ones that fit your schedule.
  3. Talk to the family, agree on pay and hours, and start working for them directly.

What's free

Right now in Orlando, Famyle is free for families and for professionals: no subscription, no fees and no commission on wages.

What Famyle does

  • Shows your job to local professionals and helps you get in touch with them.
  • Publishes guides on pay, taxes and hiring rules in Florida, like household employer taxes and our housekeeper hiring checklist.
  • Answers questions by e-mail at suporte@famyle.com, in English, Portuguese or Spanish.

What Famyle does not do

  • No background checks today. Famyle does not run background checks in the U.S. Families decide what to check before hiring.
  • Not an employer or staffing agency. The professional works for you, not for Famyle. You set the pay and the schedule, and any employer taxes are yours (see nanny tax in Florida).
  • No reviews or ratings yet in the U.S., so references from other families matter.
  • No English app yet. Everything works on the website, on your phone or computer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Famyle free?

Yes. In Orlando, families post jobs for free and professionals join and apply for free. There is no subscription and no commission on wages.

Does Famyle employ the nannies and housekeepers?

No. Famyle is not an employer or staffing agency. The family hires the professional directly and agrees on pay and schedule.

Does Famyle do background checks?

Not today. Families can check references, search Florida's public sex offender registry and, with written permission, order a background check from a screening company following federal rules.

Which areas of Orlando does Famyle cover?

West Orlando: Winter Garden and Horizon West, Windermere, Dr. Phillips and MetroWest, plus nearby areas.
