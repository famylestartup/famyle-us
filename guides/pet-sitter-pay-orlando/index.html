How Much Do Pet Sitters Make in Orlando, FL? (2026)

Hourly pay for pet sitters and dog walkers in Orlando, what families pay per visit, and how to find pet sitting clients.

Quick answer: Pet sitters in Orlando, FL typically charge $16.37–$21.83 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026); the BLS median for animal caretakers in the Orlando metro is $16.74 per hour (May 2025). Many charge per 30-minute visit or per walk, with a flat rate for overnight stays.

Per visit, per walk, per night

Many pet sitters charge per visit (a 30-minute drop-in) or per walk instead of per hour, and a flat rate for overnight stays. Agree on what's included: feeding, walks, medication, plants and mail.

What raises a pet sitter's pay

  • Experience with large dogs, puppies or senior pets.
  • Giving medication or insulin.
  • Overnight availability, holidays and the theme-park travel season.
  • Reliable transportation for back-to-back visits.

Frequently asked questions

How much do pet sitters make an hour in Orlando?

Families typically pay $16.37–$21.83 an hour for pet sitting in Orlando (Care.com, November 2026). The BLS median for animal caretakers in the Orlando metro is $16.74 an hour (May 2025).

Do pet sitters charge per hour or per visit?

Both are common. Many charge per 30-minute visit or per walk, and a flat rate for overnight stays.
