Private Cook and Personal Chef Pay in Florida (2026)

What private household cooks earn in Florida, what affects the rate, and how families in Orlando hire a cook for weekly meals.

Quick answer: Private household cooks in Florida earn a median of $17.43 per hour (BLS, May 2025). Personal chefs who plan menus and shop usually charge more, often per session with groceries paid separately. Families in Orlando use private cooks for weekly meal prep, a few hours a day, or events.

The BLS doesn't publish a separate figure for the Orlando metro. Personal chefs who plan menus and shop usually charge more than the median, often per session plus groceries.

How families use a private cook

  • Weekly meal prep: one session a week, meals for 4–5 days.
  • Daily cook: a few hours a day, often combined with light housekeeping.
  • Events: a flat rate per dinner or party.

What affects the rate

  • Diets and cuisines (gluten-free, Brazilian, Latin, kosher).
  • Who shops and who pays for groceries.
  • Cleaning the kitchen afterwards.
