Maid Service in Orlando, FL: What It Costs and How to Hire a Cleaner Directly (2026)

What a maid service in Orlando does, typical hourly rates, and the difference between booking a cleaning company and hiring an independent house cleaner directly.

Quick answer: A maid service is a cleaning company that sends its own staff to your home. In Orlando, FL, house cleaning typically costs $20.25–$25.07 per hour in 2026 (Care.com, November 2026); a regular cleaning of a 3-bedroom home takes 3–4 hours. You can also hire an independent house cleaner directly: on Famyle you post your job for free, local cleaners apply, and you choose and pay the cleaner yourself.

What to agree on before the first visit

  • Rooms and tasks included (kitchen, bathrooms, floors, laundry, inside the fridge or oven).
  • How often: weekly, every two weeks or one-time.
  • Hourly rate or a fixed price per visit, and how you pay.
  • Who brings supplies and equipment.
  • What happens on a holiday, a sick day or a cancellation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Famyle a maid service?

No. Famyle is an online platform where Orlando families post a job and independent house cleaners apply. You hire and pay the cleaner directly.

How much does a maid service cost per visit in Orlando?

At typical Orlando house cleaning rates of $20.25–$25.07 per hour (Care.com, November 2026), a 3–4 hour regular cleaning of a 3-bedroom home comes to roughly $61–$100 (3 hours at $20.25 to 4 hours at $25.07). Deep cleans and first visits take longer.

Is a house cleaner I hire directly my employee?

An independent cleaner who runs their own business, brings their own supplies and serves other clients is generally not your household employee (IRS Publication 926). A cleaner who works on your schedule under your direction may be.
