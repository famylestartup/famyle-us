What a pet sitter does

  • Feeding, fresh water and medication
  • Dog walks and playtime
  • Litter boxes and cleanup
  • Overnight stays in your home while you travel
  • Sending updates and photos

What to ask before you hire

  • Experience with your type of pet and its size or breed
  • How many visits per day and how long each one lasts
  • How they handle medications or special needs
  • What they'd do in a vet emergency
  • References from other pet owners

Hiring legally in Florida

Many pet sitters work independently and serve several clients, so they're usually not your household employee. Agree on rates, keys and emergency contacts in writing. See our guide: how to hire a household employee in Florida.
