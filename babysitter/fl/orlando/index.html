What a babysitter does

  • Watching your children for a few hours
  • Dinner, bath and bedtime routines
  • Play and supervised activities
  • Occasional school pick-up

What to ask before you hire

  • Experience with children the same age as yours
  • CPR and first-aid certification
  • Availability on the days you usually need help
  • References from other families
  • How they'd handle an emergency

Hiring legally in Florida

Occasional babysitting can still count as household employment. In 2026 Social Security and Medicare taxes apply only if you pay one person $3,000 or more in the year, and wages paid to babysitters under 18 generally don't count unless babysitting is their main job (IRS Publication 926).
