What a house cleaner does

  • Kitchen: counters, appliances outside and in, sink
  • Bathrooms: toilets, showers, tubs, mirrors
  • Dusting, vacuuming and mopping all rooms
  • Changing bed linens
  • Deep cleaning or move-in/move-out cleaning, if you agree on it

What to ask before you hire

  • Hourly rate or flat price per visit, and how long a visit takes for a home your size
  • Whether they bring their own supplies and equipment
  • References from current clients
  • What happens if they can't come on a scheduled day
  • Whether they work independently (their own business) or for a company

Hiring legally in Florida

Who counts as your employee depends on control. A cleaner from a company, or an independent cleaner who runs their own business, brings their own supplies and serves the public, is generally not your household employee (IRS Publication 926). A cleaner who works on your schedule under your direction may be. See our guide: how to hire a household employee in Florida.
